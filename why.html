Why this exists

Most systems people rely on were designed for a slower world. Education assumed stable careers. Employment assumed human labor would remain essential. Governments assumed change would be incremental.

That assumption is collapsing.

Artificial intelligence does not arrive evenly. It concentrates power, capital, and leverage into fewer hands. Those without buffers — especially young people in countries like Pakistan — absorb the shock first.

Wizmn Hub exists to give people space to think before reacting. To share ideas without branding. To prepare without panic.
